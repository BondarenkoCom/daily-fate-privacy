Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · App: Daily Fate (Android)

Daily Fate does not require an account. The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect and share device and usage information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, including during testing.

1. Who we are

Daily Fate is an Android application published under the developer name Aya Labs. For privacy questions, contact zombiemen1993@gmail.com.

2. Information collected

Daily Fate does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number. The app itself stores some preferences and daily-card progress locally on your device.

Daily Fate integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including for testing advertisements. According to Google's SDK disclosure, this SDK may automatically collect and share the following information: your device's IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), app interactions (such as app launches and taps), performance and diagnostic data (such as app launch time and stability), and device or account identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID when available). You can reset or delete the Advertising ID using Android's settings.

3. Advertising, sharing and purposes

Information described above may be processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide and measure advertisements, analyze performance, and prevent fraud or abuse. The current build uses Google's test advertising identifiers; test advertising may be displayed during testing. Aya Labs does not operate a separate user-account database or sell personal information directly.

For details of Google's handling of data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. Local app data

Daily Fate stores local preferences and daily ritual progress on your device. These app preferences are not sent to Aya Labs servers. You can clear locally stored data in Android settings or uninstall the app; doing so may remove saved preferences and progress.

5. Retention, deletion and security

Aya Labs does not maintain a server-side database of Daily Fate user profiles. Local app data can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it. Information processed by Google through its SDK is subject to Google's retention, security, and deletion practices. Google states that the data automatically collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted during transmission. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at the email address below.

6. Other platform services

Google Play and Android may also process information when you download, install or use apps, under their own terms and privacy policies. Daily Fate's Google Mobile Ads integration is separately described above.

7. Audience and children's privacy

Daily Fate is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed for children. It does not ask users to submit personal information. If you have concerns about a child's information, please contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, SDKs, or data practices change. We will publish the revised policy at this page and update the effective date.
